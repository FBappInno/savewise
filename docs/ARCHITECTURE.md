# SaveWise Architecture

## Vision

SaveWise is a personal AI knowledge companion that helps people remember, connect and rediscover valuable information from across the digital world.

The goal is not to replace human memory, but to strengthen it.

---

# Core Principles

- Privacy first
- User always stays in control
- AI assists, never decides
- Platform independent
- Knowledge instead of bookmarks
- Context instead of keywords

---

# Core Domain

Everything inside SaveWise revolves around one central concept:

## KnowledgeItem

A KnowledgeItem represents one piece of knowledge.

Examples:

- Instagram post
- Facebook post
- YouTube video
- Web article
- PDF
- Podcast
- Note
- Image
- Screenshot

Regardless of its source, SaveWise treats every piece of information equally.

---

## KnowledgeSpace

KnowledgeSpaces organize related knowledge.

Examples:

- AI
- Home
- Travel
- Finance
- Health

One KnowledgeItem may belong to multiple KnowledgeSpaces.

---

## Source

Represents where knowledge originated.

Examples:

- Instagram
- Facebook
- X
- Reddit
- LinkedIn
- Browser
- Local File

---

## Memory

Memory is the intelligence layer.

It connects KnowledgeItems together.

Responsibilities:

- AI summary
- Semantic understanding
- Related content
- Embeddings
- Reminder suggestions
- Knowledge graph

---

## Future Components

- AI Assistant
- Browser Extension
- Mobile App
- Desktop App
- Sync Service
- Search Engine
- Recommendation Engine

---

# Guiding Principle

SaveWise should feel like an extension of human memory—not a replacement for it.
